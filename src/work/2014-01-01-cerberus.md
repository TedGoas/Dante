I created **Cerberus**, an open source email framework, as an opportunity to scratch my own itch for building responsive HTML emails that worked consistently across clients. What started as a side project grew into one of the most widely used email frameworks on GitHub. During my time at Stack Overflow, I also used it to build the first ever email design system. By the time I sold it in 2025, the repo had over 5K stars, earned me a few conference speaking opportunities, and connected me more deeply with the email development community.
