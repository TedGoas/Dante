I joined Dialpad because I believe customer support teams deserve better tools. These teams do unglamorous work, are always asked to do more with less, and are usually stuck with outdated software. I wanted to help build products that make their jobs easier and their work more valued.

At Dialpad, I started as the first designer on the company’s support product and quickly grew into a leadership IC role. I’ve led several 0→1 initiatives, guided product integrations after three acquisitions, and now manage a team of eight designers. Together, we’re reimagining customer support with AI workflows that meaningfully improve the lives of agents and supervisors.

Some highlights: helping grow the support product from $50M to $300M ARR, shaping a design team that scaled alongside that growth, and delivering measurable results in the product. For example, our chatbot now deflects 62% of incoming requests, beating the industry’s then-leading benchmark of 59%.
