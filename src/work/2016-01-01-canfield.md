I joined Canfield Scientific because I was drawn to the opportunity to design technology that directly supports healthcare and clinical research. The company was known for its advanced imaging hardware, and I wanted to help bring the same level of quality and usability to the software side.

As Canfield’s first software designer, I split my time between designing tools for clinical trials in an agency model and building software for the company’s own products. I worked directly with the CEO, President, and engineers, contributing across the entire stack of design needs—from logos and marketing sites to mobile UIs and HTML emails.

During my time there, I established the foundation for Canfield’s design practice and helped shape how software supported both the company’s hardware products and its clients’ research efforts. It was a chance to be a true generalist, building design into the DNA of the company from the ground up.
